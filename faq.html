Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask about a Personal Paparazzi session, in one place. If yours is not here, the app can reach us.

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

What is Personal Paparazzi?
An on-demand photo and video service. Think of it like a rideshare, but for capturing memories: you open the app somewhere worth remembering, send a request, and a verified local nearby comes and takes the photos.
How do I get a free photo session?
Download the app and request a session. Your first one is automatically free, with no promo code and no credit card required.
Which cities is Personal Paparazzi in?
We are starting in Nashville. More cities are coming, and the app is worth having before we reach yours.
Do I need to book a photographer in advance?
No. There is no appointment and no calendar. You request a session when you are already standing somewhere worth photographing, and it goes to every Personal Paparazzi nearby.

What it costs

How much does a photo session cost?
$19.99 for a session, which is nine photos and one short video. Your first session is free.
Does Personal Paparazzi charge a subscription or monthly fee?
No. You only pay when you use the service. There are no required subscriptions, memberships, or recurring charges.
How do I pay for a photo session?
After your first free session, payment is handled securely in the app. There is nothing to hand over in person.

On the day

How long does a photo session take?
The session itself typically takes less than five minutes, from meeting your Personal Paparazzi to the last shot.
Who takes the photos?
An enthusiastic local with a good eye and a modern phone camera. Our Personal Paparazzi are not necessarily professional photographers, and every one of them passes an identity verification before they can accept sessions.
Can I ask for particular shots?
Yes. Say what you want in the frame before they start, and ask for another go if a shot is not right. They will take more than nine so you have a choice.
What if it rains, or I change my mind?
You request a session when you want one, so there is nothing to cancel in advance. If the weather turns, wait and send the request later.

Your photos

What do I get in a session?
Nine photos and one short video. Each photo or video is a memory in the app, so a session is ten memories in total.
How soon do I get my photos after the session?
The system uploads them automatically at the end of the session. You can download them to your gallery straight away.
Who owns the photos?
You do. Photos and videos captured during a session are delivered to you through the platform, and Personal Paparazzi, Inc. retains no rights to use them for commercial purposes without your explicit consent.

Still wondering? The first session answers most of it.

Nine photos and one short video for $19.99.
