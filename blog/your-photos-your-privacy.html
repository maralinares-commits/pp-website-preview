Your photos, your privacy

Handing your camera to somebody is an act of trust, and it is worth knowing what happens next.

The photos are yours

What you get from a session belongs to you. Personal Paparazzi, Inc. keeps no right to use your photos for commercial purposes without your explicit consent, and that is in the terms rather than only in a blog post.

They come to you, not to a gallery

The photos upload at the end of the session and you download them to your own phone. There is no public album with your face in it.

What you can ask for

You can ask for a shot to be retaken. You can say you would rather not be photographed from a particular angle. You can say when you are done. The person holding the camera is there for five minutes and the photos are the only reason.

What we check

Every Personal Paparazzi passes an identity verification before they can accept sessions: we confirm they are who they say they are. We do not run criminal background checks, and we will not claim otherwise.
