How to be in your own vacation photos

If you are the one who always takes the picture, you are in none of them. Four ways to fix that on your next trip.

4 September 2026

How to be in your own vacation photos

The short version

  • If you are the one always holding the camera, you are in none of the album.
  • Decide before the trip that there will be photos with you in them.
  • Ask for a variety: three relaxed, three at your best, three where everybody goes a bit mad.
  • Then hand the camera to somebody whose only job for those five minutes is the photo.

Every family has one. The person who frames everybody up, counts to three, and then is missing from the entire album.

Plan for great memories

Decide before the trip that there will be photos with you in them. It sounds obvious. It is the step everybody skips.

Pick the moment, not just the landmark

The monument will still be there in a hundred years. You at this age, with these people, on this day, will not. Pick the moments as well as the places: the table you all sat around, the street you got lost on, the two minutes before the show started.

Ask for a variety of poses

Nine photos is enough for three of each. Three relaxed, the way you actually stand. Three at your best, the model pose. And three where everybody goes a bit mad, which are usually the ones you keep.

Get somebody else to take them

A request in the app takes a few seconds, and you end up in the photographs of your own trip.
