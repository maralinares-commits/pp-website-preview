How to be in your own holiday photos

The short version

  • If you are the one always holding the camera, you are in none of the album.
  • Decide before the trip that there will be photos with you in them.
  • Aim for the walk back and the laughing rather than the posed shot at the monument.
  • Movement beats standing still: walk towards the camera, turn around, talk to each other.

Every family has one. The person who frames everybody up, counts to three, and then is missing from the entire album.

Put the phone down first

Decide before the trip that there will be photos with you in them. It sounds obvious. It is the step everybody skips.

Pick the moment, not the landmark

The photos people keep are rarely the posed one at the monument. They are the walk back, the laughing, the ice cream. Aim for those.

Ask for movement

Standing still and smiling makes everybody look like a school portrait. Walk towards the camera. Turn around. Talk to each other.

Get somebody else to take them

A request in the app takes a few seconds, and you end up in the photographs of your own holiday.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get in my own holiday photos?
Decide before the trip that there will be photos with you in them, then hand the camera to somebody else: a travelling companion, or a verified local through the app.
What makes a holiday photo worth keeping?
Usually not the posed shot at the landmark. The photos people keep are the walk back, the laughing and the ice cream, taken while something is actually happening.
How do I stop looking stiff in photos?
Move. Walk towards the camera, turn around, or talk to whoever you are with. Standing still and smiling makes everybody look like a school portrait.
