How much can you make in a weekend

7 September 2026

How much can you make in a weekend

The honest answer is that it depends on how many requests you accept and how busy it is where you are standing. That is not a dodge, so here is the arithmetic underneath it.

What one session pays

A client pays $19.99 for a session: nine photos and one short video. You keep 50% of that, which is $10.00. Any tip is yours in full, on top. Earnings are deposited weekly, once the client receives their images.

What a session costs you

Minutes. The session itself typically takes less than five. There is no gas to buy, no wear on your tires, and nothing to pay before you start, which is the part that separates this from driving.

Doing the sum

At a busy spot, an estimated 6 to 10 sessions an hour is $60 to $100 an hour before tips. At a quieter one, an estimated 3 to 5 sessions is $30 to $50. Over a Saturday and a Sunday, eight hours each, the same arithmetic runs from a few hundred dollars to something over a thousand.

The part we cannot promise

Those sessions an hour are estimated from how busy each place is. They are not measured, they are not an offer, and they are not a commitment to pay any amount. Nobody can tell you in advance how many people will press the button while you are standing there.

What we can tell you is the rate, which is fixed, and the time, which is short. The calculator on the sign-up page runs the sum for whichever place and however many hours you want to try.
