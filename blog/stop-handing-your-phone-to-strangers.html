Stop handing your phone to strangers

The short version

  • A stranger doing you a favour takes one rushed photo from where they were already standing.
  • The place you travelled to ends up as a smudge behind your head.
  • If you do ask, ask before you hand the phone over: where to stand, what should be behind you, and three shots rather than one.
  • Or send a request in the app and let somebody who does this on purpose take them.

You are somewhere worth remembering. You look around, pick the friendliest face, and hand over your phone.

What comes back is almost never what you wanted. The framing is tight, so the place you travelled to is a smudge behind your head. The focus is on the wall. Somebody blinked. And you cannot ask for another one without feeling rude, so you say thank you and walk away with it.

Why it goes wrong

The stranger is doing you a favour, and favours are rushed. They take one photo, not fifteen. They stand where they were already standing rather than backing up. They do not know that the fountain behind you is the whole point.

What to do instead

Ask for the shot you want before you hand the phone over. Say where to stand. Say what should be in the frame behind you. Ask for three, not one.

Or send a request in the app and let somebody who does this on purpose take them.
