Who are the Personal Paparazzi?

This is who we're looking for.

Our Personal Paparazzi aren't necessarily professional photographers, just as Uber drivers weren't professional drivers before joining the platform.

They're enthusiastic locals who have a good eye and know how to use a modern phone camera.

They go through a registration and identity verification before they can accept shoots.

And they know where people will want to capture a memory, so they're already nearby. If that sounds like you, keep reading.

How it works

Three steps, and two of them happen on your phone.

  1. 1

    Be nearby

    Position yourself near popular spots: landmarks, parks, event venues, anywhere people will want great photos.

  2. 2

    Accept a shoot

    When a request comes in, accept it and the app guides both you and the client to meet. Shoot 9 photos and a short video.

  3. 3

    Get paid

    Photos upload automatically. Once the client receives their images, your $10.00 share plus the whole tip is deposited weekly directly into your account.

What does a Personal Paparazzi do?

When someone nearby opens the app and requests a shoot, you accept, meet them at their location, and take 9 photos and a short video. The shoot itself typically takes less than five minutes.

Questions local creators ask

Do I need to be a professional photographer?
No. Our Personal Paparazzi aren't necessarily professional photographers, just as Uber drivers weren't professional drivers before joining the platform. You need a good eye and a modern phone camera.
What do I get paid?
You keep 50% of the price: $10.00 a memory, and 100% of any tip. Earnings are deposited weekly, once the client receives their images.
How long does one shoot take?
The shoot itself typically takes less than five minutes. You take nine photos and one short video, and they upload automatically.
What equipment do I need?
A modern smartphone with a good camera. No professional equipment is required, and we'll provide tips and guidance to help you deliver great results.
Do I have to work set hours?
No. Work whenever you want, with no set schedule. Position yourself near popular spots and accept the requests you want.
What checks do I have to pass?
An identity verification: we confirm you are who you say you are. You also need to be 18 or older and to complete a short onboarding process.
