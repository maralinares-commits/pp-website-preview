Become a Personal Paparazzi

Turn your eye for great photos into flexible income, on your schedule, in your city.

You keep 50%: $10.00 a session, plus every tip

Who we're looking for

No studio, no training, no portfolio. This is the whole list.

  • You take good photos on your phone. That is the skill. Our Personal Paparazzi aren't necessarily professional photographers, in the same way rideshare drivers weren't professional drivers before joining.
  • You know your own city. You know where people want their picture taken, and where the light is good at six in the evening.
  • You're comfortable with strangers. A client who feels relaxed takes a better photo, and putting people at ease for five minutes is most of the job.
  • You're already out and about. This is gig work on foot, so there is no gas to buy, no wear on your tires, and nothing to pay before you start. Being nearby is worth more than being fast.
  • You're 18 or older, with a modern phone. You pass an identity check before you can accept sessions, and that is the only check there is.

How it works

Three steps, and two of them happen on your phone.

  1. 1

    Be nearby

    Position yourself near popular spots: landmarks, parks, event venues, anywhere people will want great photos.

  2. 2

    Accept a session

    When a request comes in, accept it and the app guides both you and the client to meet. Take 9 photos and a short video.

  3. 3

    Get paid

    Photos upload automatically. Once the client receives their images, your $10.00 share plus the whole tip is deposited weekly directly into your account.

What does a Personal Paparazzi do?

You take nine photos and one short video. That is the job.

A request comes in from someone standing a few streets away. You accept it, the app walks you to them, and you introduce yourself.

Then you do the part that matters. You step back far enough to get the place in the frame with them, not just their faces. You wait for the second where nobody is blinking. You ask them to walk towards you, or turn around, or laugh at something, because people look like themselves when they are moving. You take more than you need so they have a choice.

Nine photos, one short video, and it usually takes less than five minutes. Then you tell them you're done, check they're happy, and the photos upload on their own while you walk away.

You don't need to be a professional photographer, just someone with a good eye and a modern phone. We'll provide tips and guidance to help you deliver great results every time.

Questions before your first gig

Do I need to be a professional photographer?
No. Our Personal Paparazzi aren't necessarily professional photographers, in the same way rideshare drivers weren't professional drivers before joining. You need a good eye and a modern phone camera.
What do I get paid?
You keep 50% of the price: $10.00 a session, and 100% of any tip. Earnings are deposited weekly, once the client receives their images.
How long does one session take?
The session itself typically takes less than five minutes. You take nine photos and one short video, and they upload automatically.
What equipment do I need?
A modern smartphone with a good camera. No professional equipment is required, and we'll provide tips and guidance to help you deliver great results.
Do I have to work set hours?
No. Work whenever you want, with no set schedule. Position yourself near popular spots and accept the requests you want.
What checks do I have to pass?
An identity verification: we confirm you are who you say you are. You also need to be 18 or older and to complete a short onboarding process.
